Introduction

Thank you for your interest in participating in this research project. The following few pages will provide you with further information about the project, so that you can decide if you would like to take part in this research.

Please take the time to read this information carefully. You may ask questions about anything you don’t understand or want to know more about.

Your participation is voluntary. If you don’t wish to take part, you don’t have to. If you begin participating, you can also stop at any time.

What is this research about?

This research project investigates how planning before a writing task affects English writing performance. In particular, we are interested in whether having time to think about and organise ideas before writing influences the way people complete an English writing task.

The study focuses on Chinese international students studying in Australia. Participants will complete English writing tasks under different planning conditions. For example, before some writing tasks, you may be given time to plan your ideas and make notes. For other tasks, you may be asked to begin writing with little or no planning time.

We will examine different aspects of the writing process and the written texts, such as how ideas are organised, how quickly the text is produced, and how language is used. The findings may help researchers better understand the role of pre-task planning in second-language writing and may inform the design of English language learning and teaching activities.

What will I be asked to do?

You will be asked to take part in several research activities. We will arrange the sessions at a suitable time for you. The activities do not necessarily need to be completed in one sitting.

First, you will complete a questionnaire about your language background and your experience of learning and using English. This will take approximately 15 minutes.

The main part of the study will involve completing a series of English writing tasks on a computer. Before some tasks, you will be given time to think about the topic, organise your ideas, and make planning notes. In other tasks, you may be asked to begin writing immediately or after a shorter planning period. The writing activities will take approximately 45 minutes to 1 hour.

The computer software will record your written responses. It may also record information about the writing process, such as when you type, pause, revise, or delete parts of your text. We are interested in general patterns of writing behaviour rather than evaluating you as an individual. Here is a schematic illustration of the experiment setup.

What are the possible benefits?

Participating in this study offers you a unique opportunity to reflect on and enhance your English language learning journey. As you share your experiences, challenges, and successes, you'll gain deeper insights into your own learning strategies and areas for improvement. This process of reflection can significantly boost your self-awareness and renew your motivation to master English. To compensate for your time, all participants will receive a little gift after completing the activities.

Additionally, you might discover new learning resources and effective strategies through interactions with researchers and potentially other participants. Such insights can be invaluable in shaping a more effective and enjoyable language learning experience. Moreover, your contribution to this study will help improve English language education for future learners, giving you a sense of accomplishment and connection to a wider community of language learners.

You may also receive personalized feedback on your writing skills, offering a clear direction for your future efforts. Beyond the academic benefits, engaging in this study can enhance your cultural awareness and confidence in using English in a variety of settings. It's not just about the language; it's an opportunity for personal growth and becoming part of an effort to make learning English a more supportive and effective experience for everyone involved.

What are the possible risks?

We want to assure you that the risks associated with participating in this study are minimal. The methods we're using are widely accepted in linguistic research, and many of the activities, such as language background questionnaire, will be familiar to you based on your existing experiences with learning English. We understand that joining this study means dedicating about 1 hour of your time to our activities.

We also acknowledge that some parts of the study might feel repetitive or lead to fatigue. To help with this, we will schedule breaks between different sections of the experiment, giving you time to rest. It's important to us that you have a comfortable and positive experience during the study. Your participation is entirely voluntary, and if at any point you choose not to continue, you can opt out without any repercussions. Our aim is to make this study not only valuable in terms of the insights it generates about language learning but also an engaging and accommodating experience for you.

Do I have to take part?

No. Your decision to participate in this study is completely voluntary, meaning you have the freedom to join without any pressure or obligation. We want to ensure you're fully informed about the study's aims, what it involves, your rights as a participant, and any potential risks or benefits. This way, your choice to be part of our research is made with a clear understanding of what to expect.

If at any point you decide this study isn't right for you, you can withdraw your participation immediately. Your decision to leave will not affect your access to services or rights in any way. Should you choose to withdraw, you also have the option to decide what happens with the data collected from you up to that point — whether it can still be used or if you prefer it to be deleted.

To support your voluntary participation, we are committed to maintaining a transparent, respectful, and ethical research environment. Your privacy and confidentiality are paramount, and all personal information will be protected and used solely for the purposes outlined. We encourage open communication and invite you to reach out to us at any stage if you have questions, need more information, or decide to withdraw from the study.

Additionally, we can provide regular updates and feedback about the study's progress and findings. Our aim is to make your participation in this study a positive and enriching experience, fully respecting your rights and well-being throughout the process.

Will I hear about the results of this project?

The insights gained from this study are invaluable, not only to our understanding of language learning, English writing processes but also to the broader academic community and future educational strategies. Our findings will be meticulously analysed and used for scholarly research, with the ultimate goal of publishing our results in academic journals and presenting them at relevant conferences.

Once these results are officially published, we will prepare a summary report. This report will distil our findings into easy-to-understand language, ensuring you can grasp the key outcomes and implications of the study without needing to navigate through academic terminologies. We are committed to keeping you informed and will make this summary available to you, acknowledging your crucial role in this research.

What will happen to information about me?

We take the privacy of your information very seriously in our study. All the data we collect from you will be kept safe on a university computer, which only the research team can access because it's password-protected. After we've published our findings, we'll hold onto the data for five more years just in case we need to look back at it, but then we'll securely delete everything. This means that your information will be kept private and used only for this research project, and you can be sure that we're doing everything we can to protect your confidentiality. All data collected in the project will be used only for this research project (i.e. no future use).

Where can I get further information?

If you would like more information about the project, please contact Dr Yizhou Wang at yizwang3@unimelb.edu.au.

Will participation affect my success or grades?

No. Participating in the current study is completely voluntary and it will NOT affect your coursework or research achievements at the University of Melbourne.
